## PbSe/Cr source article

The processed PbSe/Cr coordinates were reconstructed from figures in:

S. Liu *et al.*, "Ultralow chromium doping enables all-PbSe thermoelectric
cooling," *Science* **392**, 1056-1060 (2026),
DOI: [10.1126/science.aeg8963](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aeg8963).

The article PDF, Supplementary Information PDF, and source-figure images are not
redistributed. `data/raw/source_records.csv` records their source identifiers
and SHA-256 values. The processed coordinates remain derived data and should be
used together with a citation to the source article.
